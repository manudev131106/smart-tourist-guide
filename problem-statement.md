Problem Statement

When I visit a new city or tourist destination, I often do not know which places I should visit, how to reach them, how much time I should spend at each place, or what facilities are available nearby. I usually have to search through different websites and applications to find information about tourist attractions, restaurants, hotels, transportation, opening hours, ticket prices, and other useful details. This makes trip planning time-consuming and confusing.

Another problem is that I may have limited time to explore a city. I need to decide which places can realistically be visited within my available time and in what order I should visit them. Manually planning routes can result in unnecessary travelling, wasted time, or missing important places. Information such as opening hours, weather conditions, and travel times can also affect my plans.

I need a single system where I can search for tourist places, view useful information about them, find nearby places, and get directions. The system should also help me create a suitable itinerary based on the places I want to visit and the time available to me. It should be able to recommend relevant places according to my interests and provide updated information whenever possible.

The system should reduce the need to use multiple applications while travelling. Instead, I should be able to discover places, understand their details, plan my trip, navigate between destinations, save places I am interested in, and receive useful reminders from one platform.

Therefore, the Smart Tourist Guide System is proposed to make travelling easier and more organized by providing tourists with destination information, recommendations, itinerary planning, nearby-place discovery, and navigation in a single system.
